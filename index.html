How it works

A clear path from first call to final walkthrough

  1. 01

    Consult

    We walk the space, listen to what you need, and map constraints— budget, timeline, and the life of the home.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Scope, materials, and sequencing are written down so everyone knows what “done” looks like before work begins.

  3. 03

    Build

    Construction moves with care—protected floors, tidy sites, and updates when decisions need your eye.

  4. 04

    Finish

    Detail work, punch list, and a final walkthrough. We don’t leave until the craft meets the plan.
