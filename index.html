How a project runs

From first visit to final walkthrough

A simple path so you always know where the job stands.

  1. STEP 01

    Consult

    Site visit, goals, constraints, budget range, and timeline. No hard sell—just a clear read on the work.

  2. STEP 02

    Proposal

    Written scope, approach, and next steps so you can compare apples to apples before you commit.

  3. STEP 03

    Build

    Construction with protection, sequencing, and check-ins when choices come up behind the drywall.

  4. STEP 04

    Finish & handoff

    Detail work, punch list, and walkthrough. We don’t walk away until the agreed work is complete.
